# The optimal page flow (v2, 2026-10-28): engagement first, conversion as the next step

**Principles** (CRO practice; evidence in `GUIDES-CONVERSION.md`):
1. **Answer her search in the first screen,** in plain friendly words. Trust is earned before any ask.
2. **Then help with the NEXT thing she will need,** using real finds. The app is offered as "more of exactly this, for you".
3. **Every block is built for THIS page's reader.** A courthouse bride gets short or simple dresses, a small bouquet and a lunch spot, never a ball gown, a barn or a 100-seat room.
4. **One button promise per page, in the first person,** naming the outcome she just saw:
   - "Show me courthouse dresses in my size";
   - "Show me gowns in my size";
   - "Find my dress & decor for less".
   Where it appears: once after the first helpful block, once at the end, plus a phone-only bar once she has scrolled past the answer. A small header link. Nothing else.
5. **The price, plainly, next to the button:** "Free: your board and your first finds · every new find each week: $9.92/month billed yearly, or $24/month · no card to start".
6. **Show the product without disclaimers in large type.** No "AI-made", no blur notes in the body: a one-line caption in small print is enough. No statistics that don't help her decide.
7. **Sources:** one small "official sources" or "where these prices come from" link per block, never repeated on every row. "How we check" lives in the footer.
8. **Engagement tools that keep her on the page:** filter chips (size, guests, budget), "open more" lists, a short tips block, related guides.
9. **Scannable on a phone:**
   - each card shows at most 3 lines;
   - numbered steps;
   - no wall of 17 rows;
   - nothing cut off, no sideways scroll.

## The flow by page type
**Courthouse (5):**
1. H1, then the answer in 3 lines: total fees · where · days and booking ("$70 in fees · 119 W. Randolph St. · Tue–Fri, no appointment"). Then "last checked".
2. **"Do it in 5 steps":** numbered and short, with each step's fee. Sources behind one small link.
3. **"Your courthouse look for less":** 4 real items, each checked live:
   - 2 short or simple white dresses under $250 (from the pool: midi, short, slip, simple, jumpsuit);
   - a small bouquet from a local florist's own page;
   - a 1-hour courthouse photo session (from the photographer's own page, or omitted);
   - a nearby lunch or dinner spot for 6–10 people (from the city venue page).
4. **The button block:**
   - "Show me courthouse dresses in my size", with the price line;
   - 3 steps (tap your style · Rachel searches · new finds every Tuesday);
   - the real board image in a clean phone frame.
5. **"Make the day feel like a wedding":** 5 short tips (what to wear, best time to go, photo spots nearby, who to bring, where to celebrate after). General advice is fine; any fact needs its source.
6. FAQ (People also ask).
7. Related: this city's small venues, packages.
8. The end button.

**Venues (5):**
1. The answer: "11 small venues in Houston with posted prices: from $197 to $8,900 (10–50 guests), checked Oct 28".
2. Filter chips: guests (≤ 20 · ≤ 50 · 50+) and budget (< $1k · < $3k · any).
3. Venue cards, cheapest first: name, the price as quoted, guests, what's included, day limits, link.
4. The button after the 4th card: "Venue sorted? Rachel finds your dress & decor for less".
5. "How to pay less for a small venue": weekday, off-season, hire-only vs packages, questions to ask.
6. The courthouse option for this city, plus packages.
7. FAQ, then the end button.

**Dresses (2):**
1. The answer with the count and range.
2. Size chips (2–4 · 6–8 · 10–12 · 14+) that filter the grid.
3. A 2-column grid: brand · model · size · price · seller's original · "View on Poshmark".
4. The button after the 6th card: "Not your size? Show me gowns in my size every week".
5. "Buying secondhand safely": measurements, buyer protection, alteration costs.
6. FAQ, then the end button.

**Packages and the hub:** the same pattern as venues, with a city chooser at the top.
**Home:** a menu of cards (courthouse · venues · dresses · packages), one answer line each, then one button block.

## The app side (not this repo)
The button opens `rachelslist.co/start?utm_…&from=<page type>`. The app does not read `from` yet. Once the owner publishes that change, a courthouse visitor will start on simple-dress styles instead of barns.
