Questions people ask

How much does a courthouse wedding cost?

In 2026, from $60 in New York City to $242 in San Francisco in official fees, license plus ceremony, across the seven cities we checked on each clerk's own page (re-read Oct 29, 2026): $60 in New York, $70 in Chicago, $126 in Los Angeles, $161 in Dallas, $181 in Houston, $236 in San Diego and $242 in San Francisco.

Which city has the cheapest courthouse wedding?

New York: $35 license + $25 ceremony = $60. Chicago is next at $70 ($60 license + $10 ceremony).

Is the courthouse wedding cost just the marriage license?

No. The totals here add the marriage license and the civil ceremony fee. The license alone runs $35 in New York to $129 in San Diego (public license) and San Francisco (public or confidential).

What else can you pay at a courthouse wedding?

Extras on the official pages (read Oct 29, 2026): in Chicago a certified copy is $15 (Cook County Clerk); a county witness is $20 in Los Angeles (LA County) and $58 in San Diego, where card payments add $2.50 (San Diego County) and a certified copy is $19 (San Diego County). In San Francisco a ceremony off site, on a weekend or on a holiday is $336 (San Francisco County Clerk), and a certified copy of a public marriage certificate is $19 (San Francisco Assessor-Recorder).

Can you get married the same day at the courthouse?

In San Diego, yes: the county says you can marry as soon as the license is issued. In San Francisco, no: the County Clerk does not sell the license on the day of your ceremony. New York has a 24-hour wait, Chicago 1 day, and Houston and Dallas 72 hours (in Dallas a district judge can waive it). The Los Angeles pages do not state a waiting period.
