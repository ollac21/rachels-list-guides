The steps

  1. Both of you apply in person at a Harris County Clerk office with valid photo ID (downtown: 201 Caroline, Suite 330, Mon-Fri 8:00 AM-4:30 PM); the license costs $81, or $181 if neither ID shows Texas residency.
  2. Wait 72 hours after the license is issued before the ceremony (waived for active military; a Twogether in Texas premarital-course certificate also waives it and takes up to $60 off the fee).
  3. Book a courthouse ceremony with a Harris County Justice of the Peace; Precinct 1, Place 1 charges $100 on weekday slots (cash only), $200 on Saturdays when available, and takes appointments or walk-ins at the judge's availability. Bring the license and the cash.
  4. Have the officiant sign within 90 days of the issue date, then return the signed license to the Clerk for recording (in person or by mail).

Questions people ask

How much does it cost to get married at the courthouse in Houston, TX?

As of Oct 28, 2026, the Harris County Clerk charges $81 for the marriage license ($181 if neither applicant shows Texas residency on their ID), and Justice of the Peace Precinct 1, Place 1 charges $100 for a weekday courthouse ceremony, cash only: $181 in all. Other Harris County justice courts set their own fees.

How do I get married at the courthouse in Houston?

Both of you apply in person at one of the Harris County Clerk's 11 locations with valid photo ID, then wait 72 hours. Book a ceremony with a Justice of the Peace, bring the license and cash, and return the signed license to the Clerk within 90 days.

Can you get married at the Harris County courthouse?

Yes: Harris County Justices of the Peace perform courthouse ceremonies. Precinct 1, Place 1 lists Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM at $100, and Saturdays 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM at $200 when available.

Can you get a marriage license and get married in the same day in Texas?

Not by default: Texas requires a 72-hour wait after the license is issued. The Harris County Clerk's page says the wait is waived for active military and for couples with a Twogether in Texas certificate, which also takes up to $60 off the fee.

Is a courthouse wedding cheaper than a wedding?

The official fees here total $181. The lowest Houston venue price we verified for a group of 10 or more is $380 (The Middleton Venue), so the courthouse is the cheaper start; compare both pages before you decide.
