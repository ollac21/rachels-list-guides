The steps in order
  1. Both of you complete the county's marriage license application online, then book an in-person appointment (appointments open up to 3 weeks ahead).
  2. Both of you go to the appointment together with valid photo ID and pay the license fee: $91 for a public license, $85 for a confidential license.
  3. If you want the county to marry you, book a prepaid 15-minute civil ceremony ($35); the county only gives the ceremony appointment once you hold your license. A county-provided witness is $20, and a confidential license needs no witness.
  4. Marry within 90 days of getting the license.
  5. Return the signed license to the Norwalk office so it can be recorded.

Questions people ask

How much does a courthouse wedding cost in Los Angeles?

As of Oct 28, 2026, Los Angeles County charges $91 for a public marriage license ($85 for a confidential one) and $35 for a civil ceremony at a county office: $126 in all. A county-provided witness is $20 more.

Can you get married at the courthouse in Los Angeles?

The county marries you at its Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk offices: civil ceremonies are by appointment only, prepaid, and last 15 minutes. The county gives no ceremony appointment until you hold your license, and outside officiants are not allowed in its chapels.

How do you get a marriage license in Los Angeles County?

Both of you complete the application online, then book an in-person appointment at a county office (up to 3 weeks ahead) and go together with valid photo ID. The license costs $91 (public) or $85 (confidential) and is valid for 90 days.

Can you get married the same day in Los Angeles?

The county offers a 45-minute express appointment at all seven of its branch offices, which it describes as license and ceremony in one visit. You still need the application and the $126 in fees.

Is it cheaper to get married at a courthouse?

The county's fees total $126. The lowest small-venue price we verified in Los Angeles is $449.99 (Lucky Wedding Day Chapel), so the county office is the cheaper start; see both pages before you decide.
