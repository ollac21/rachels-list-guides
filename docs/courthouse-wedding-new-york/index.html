The steps

  1. Book your appointment: City Clerk offices take no walk-ins, so you make an appointment online through Project Cupid (nyc.gov/cupid) or by phone.
  2. Both of you go to a City Clerk borough office with valid photo ID and the $35 fee (card or money order); with complete documents you get the license the same day.
  3. Wait at least 24 hours after you receive the license before the ceremony (a judge's waiver is possible, no fee). The license is usually valid for 60 days.
  4. Book the City Clerk ceremony ($25) at any of the five borough offices and arrive between 8:30 AM and 3:15 PM, Monday to Friday.
  5. Bring the license, at least one witness over 18 and the same type of photo ID used for the license; no more than 6 attendees in all.
  6. After the ceremony you receive your Marriage Certificate the same day.

Questions people ask

How do you get married at a courthouse in New York City?

Make an appointment online through Project Cupid or by phone (City Clerk offices take no walk-ins), then go to a borough office with valid photo ID and the $35 fee. After the 24-hour wait, book the $25 City Clerk ceremony at any of the five borough offices and bring at least one witness over 18.

How much does a courthouse wedding cost in NYC?

As of Oct 28, 2026, the City Clerk charges $35 for the marriage license and $25 for the civil ceremony, $60 in all. Both figures come from the City's own NYC311 pages linked in the table.

How many people are allowed in a courthouse wedding in NYC?

The City Clerk's ceremony page says no more than 6 attendees, including the couple and the witness. For a bigger party, see the small-venue prices on our NYC venues page, from $595.

How quickly can I get married in NYC?

With complete documents you leave the City Clerk office with the $35 license the same day, but the ceremony must be at least 24 hours after you receive it. A judicial waiver from the County Clerk can allow a ceremony inside that wait, with no fee.

Is it cheaper to get married at a courthouse?

The City Clerk's fees total $60. The lowest price we verified for a small venue in NYC is $595 (Occasions Hall (Queens Party Hall)), so the courthouse is the cheaper start; compare both pages before you decide.
