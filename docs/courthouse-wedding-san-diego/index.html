Make the day feel like a wedding

  • Say yes outsideAt the County Administration Center, outdoor ceremonies take place in Waterfront Park, with no limit on guests. The county may move you to the East Plaza when there is an event in the park, and weather can change the plan.
  • Leave the décor at homeThe county does not allow floral arrangements for the ceremony area, arches or backdrops, live or amplified music, confetti, petals or rice, or open alcohol, champagne included.
  • Park freeThe County Administration Center has free underground parking for up to 3 hours during business hours; the garage entrance is on Ash Street.
  • Need a Saturday?One Saturday a month, at the Santee office only, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., by appointment. The next dates are November 14 and December 12, 2026.
  • Who to bringIndoor rooms hold 20 to 30 guests at the County Administration Center, 15 in Chula Vista and San Marcos and 20 in Santee (per the county's FAQ). A public license needs at least one witness.
  • What to wearA short or midi white dress, a slip dress or a jumpsuit is easy to wear for a county office ceremony and looks great in photos. Bring a light jacket or wrap for the park.

Questions people ask

How much does a courthouse wedding cost in San Diego?

As of Oct 29, 2026, the San Diego County Clerk charges $129 for a public marriage license ($144 for a confidential one) and $107 for a civil ceremony: $236 in all, or $251 with a confidential license. A county-provided witness is $58 more, and paying by card adds $2.50.

Can you get married at the courthouse in San Diego?

In San Diego the County Clerk, not a court, marries you: by appointment only, at the County Administration Center or the Chula Vista, San Marcos or Santee office. At the County Administration Center you can also marry outdoors, in Waterfront Park.

How do you get a marriage license in San Diego County?

Book an appointment (up to 7 weeks ahead), then fill in the county's online application. Both of you go together, each with a current government photo ID (an expired one is refused), and you must both be 18 or older. The license costs $129 (public) or $144 (confidential) and is valid for 90 days, with no extensions.

Can you get married the same day in San Diego?

Yes, with a “License and Ceremony” appointment: the county says you can marry as soon as the license is issued. Appointments open up to 7 weeks ahead and fill up quickly, so book early.

Do you need a witness for a San Diego courthouse wedding?

With a public license, at least one witness must be at the ceremony: bring your own, or the county provides one for $58. A confidential license needs no witness, but you must already live together as spouses to get one.
