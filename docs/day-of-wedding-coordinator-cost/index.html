Questions people ask

How much does a day-of wedding coordinator cost?

As of Oct 29, 2026, Thumbtack puts the national average at about $764 and Zola puts day-of coordination at $800 to $3,000 (updated Feb 19, 2026). Coordinators who print their own price ask $800 (Joyous Gatherings, 8 hours), $1,695 (Complete Weddings + Events, Chicago) and $1,595 to $2,695 (the same company in Seattle).

When does a day-of coordinator start working with you?

Zola says day-of coordinators start about 4 to 6 weeks before the wedding, to learn your plans, meet your vendors and build the timeline. Joyous Gatherings starts planning 4 to 8 weeks out for its $800 tier and 2 to 3 months out for its $1,000 tier.

How much is a partial or full wedding planner?

Zola puts partial planning at $1,500 to $6,000 (updated Feb 19, 2026). In Charlotte, The Charlotte Bride lists partial planning at $2,750 to $6,000 and full-service planning at $4,250 to $10,000+ (Mar 3, 2026).

How many hours does a day-of coordinator work?

Joyous Gatherings includes 8 hours of wedding-day coverage in both its $800 and $1,000 tiers. Complete Weddings + Events says its day-of packages run six to ten hours, at $1,595 to $2,695 in Seattle.

What does month-of coordination cost?

Stephanie Gatton Events, a New York City coordinator, says its month-of packages start at $3,500 and most couples invest $3,500 to $4,500; it starts five weeks before the wedding date.
