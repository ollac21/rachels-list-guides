Questions people ask

Where do your numbers come from?

Courthouse fees come from the county or city clerk's own web page and are quoted word for word. Venue and photographer prices come from their own websites. Dress prices come from the Poshmark listing, re-opened on the day shown.

Do you earn money from the venues, sellers or links on these pages?

No. There are no affiliate links, and we earn nothing from any seller or venue listed. The only button on a page leads to Rachel's List, our own free app.

What happens when a price changes?

Every row shows the day we read it. Dress pages are re-checked every Tuesday, venue pages monthly, and courthouse pages every three months. A price we can no longer confirm is removed, not guessed.

Why is a fee or a price missing from a page?

If we cannot find a number on the official or seller's own page, we leave it out and say so. Prices in our guides come from official pages or the seller's own page, never from directories. Our calculators and cost pages also show published averages (Zola, Thumbtack, the US Bureau of Labor Statistics), each one named, linked and dated, and never presented as a real price.

How do I report a wrong price?

Check the source link on the row and tell us through rachelslist.co. If the source says something different, we correct the page and the date.
