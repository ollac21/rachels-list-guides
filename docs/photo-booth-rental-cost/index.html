Questions people ask

How much does a photo booth rental cost?

As of Oct 29, 2026, Thumbtack puts the national average at $361 to $605. Check Cherry's booking data from about 118,000 rentals puts the typical event at about $681, with half of all rentals between $452 and $1,022 (updated Aug 10, 2026). Booths that publish their own prices ask $300 to $1,100 for 2 to 3 hours.

How much does a photo booth cost per hour?

Thumbtack says some companies charge $125 to $200 per hour, with packages starting at two or three hours minimum. On the booths' own pages, an extra hour costs $75 to $150.

How many hours of photo booth do I need?

Most packages here are 2 to 3 hours. In Check Cherry's data the median rental totals $495 for 2 hours or less, $699 for 3 hours, $800 for 4 hours and $993 for 5 or more hours.

Why do photo booth prices differ so much?

Booth type and prints. Check Cherry puts a classic open-air booth near $615 and a glam or mirror booth near $950. Prints add to the price: Crown Photo Booth Rental asks $800 digital and $1,100 with prints; Spread Moore Love asks $400 digital and $550 with prints.

What does a wedding photo booth package include?

Snap Savvy Booths' Wedding Package is $500 for 2 hours 30 minutes; FunFoto's is $900 for 3 hours with an on-site attendant and unlimited 2x6 prints. Crown, Spread Moore Love and SBJamesPhotography list setup and teardown, an on-site attendant and a backdrop.
