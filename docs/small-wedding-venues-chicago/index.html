How to pay less for a small venue

  • Book a weekdayMany venues print a lower price for Monday to Thursday. The Garfield Park Conservatory's Jensen Room is $400 Monday to Thursday. Cheney Mansion's weekday price is $2,900.
  • Ask for the room onlyA package adds an officiant, photos or decor you may not need, and its price shows it. Ask what the room costs on its own.
  • Book fewer hoursA ceremony and a toast fit in an hour or two. The Oak Park Conservatory rents a 2-hour ceremony-only slot for $600. First Unitarian's Hull Chapel is a 3-hour block at $1,500.
  • Ask what comes on topFees, deposits and extra hours can sit on top of the printed price. At the Jensen Room, extra hours are $100 each and a deposit of up to $500 applies. Cheney Mansion adds a $500 security deposit.
  • Ask three questionsWhich dates are open? What is the guest limit? Is there a cheaper day or a shorter slot? Put the answers in writing before you pay a deposit.

What is a small wedding considered?

There is no official cutoff, so we use each venue's own words: the guest limits printed on this page run from 30 to 100 people. Every venue here prints its price for a small, micro, weekday or elopement booking.

How much does a micro wedding cost in Chicago?

The venue prices we read run $400 (Garfield Park Conservatory: Jensen Room) to $2,900 (Cheney Mansion: weekday wedding). The low end is a ceremony-only slot; the high end is a package with more included. Food, a dress and rings are extra.

Is $5,000 enough for a wedding in Chicago?

For a small group, yes: the Garfield Park Conservatory's Jensen Room is $400 for four hours, Monday to Thursday (room for up to 100; no bookings after 3 pm), and the most expensive venue on this page is $2,900 (Cheney Mansion, weekday).
