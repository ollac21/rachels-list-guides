How to pay less for a small venue

  • Book a weekdayMany venues print a lower price for Monday to Thursday. Blush Event Garden's elopements run Monday to Friday ($250 for 30 minutes, $450 for an hour). Heirloom Hideaway's 60-minute elopement is $675 Monday to Thursday.
  • Ask for the room onlyA package adds an officiant, photos or decor you may not need, and its price shows it. Ask what the room costs on its own.
  • Book fewer hoursA ceremony and a toast fit in an hour or two. Blush Event Garden's Essential elopement is 30 minutes for $250. Drive Up & Wed's Sweet & Simple is a 30-minute chapel ceremony for $650.
  • Ask what comes on topFees, deposits and extra hours can sit on top of the printed price. Bouré Event Studio charges $150 an hour with a 4-hour minimum. The Penrose House's $2,500 package is a starting price.
  • Ask three questionsWhich dates are open? What is the guest limit? Is there a cheaper day or a shorter slot? Put the answers in writing before you pay a deposit.

Questions people ask

What is a normal cost for a wedding venue in Dallas?

The middle price of the 11 venues printed on this page is $600, and 8 of them are under $1,000 ($250 to $2,500 overall). A price covers only what the venue names: its hours, days and guest limit, so read the card before you compare.

What are some cheap wedding venues in Dallas under $1,000?

8 of the 11 venues print a price under $1,000: Skip The Courthouse Chapel: Tie The Knot $250; Blush Event Garden: Essential elopement $250; Blush Event Garden: Classic elopement $450; Skip The Courthouse Chapel: She Said Yes $550; Bouré Event Studio $600; Flourish Events: Silver micro wedding $600; Drive Up and Wed: Sweet and Simple $650; Heirloom Hideaway: Just the 2 of You $675. Prices are for the package or hours each venue names, so read the row before you book.

What is a small wedding considered?

There is no official cutoff, so we use each venue's own words: the guest limits printed on this page run from 2 to 75 people. Every venue here prints its price for a small, micro, weekday or elopement booking.

How much does a micro wedding cost in Dallas?

The venue prices we read run $250 (Skip The Courthouse Chapel: Tie The Knot) to $2,500 (The Penrose House: DIY elopement). The low end is a ceremony-only slot; the high end is a package with more included. Food, a dress and rings are extra.

Is $5,000 enough for a wedding in Dallas?

For a small group, yes: Blush Event Garden's Classic elopement is $450 for one hour with seating for up to 20, Bouré Event Studio is $600 for four hours (Monday to Thursday, 75 seated), and the most expensive venue on this page is $2,500 (The Penrose House, a starting price). Either leaves most of $5,000 for the rest of the day. A 100-guest wedding is a different budget; this page prices small groups only.
