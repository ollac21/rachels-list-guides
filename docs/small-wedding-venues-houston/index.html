How to pay less for a small venue

  • Book a weekdayMany venues print a lower price for Monday to Thursday. The Courthouse Wedding Chapel is $197 on a weekday and $297 on a weekend. Silver Sycamore's micro wedding package is $3,520 Monday to Thursday and $4,620 Friday to Sunday.
  • Ask for the room onlyA package adds an officiant, photos or decor you may not need, and its price shows it. Ask what the room costs on its own.
  • Book fewer hoursA ceremony and a toast fit in an hour or two. The Middleton Venue sells 1 hour for $380 (2 hours is $760). Houston Botanic Garden rents the Global Collection Garden for 1 hour at $500.
  • Ask what comes on topFees, deposits and extra hours can sit on top of the printed price. Silver Sycamore adds tax and a 20% service fee to its package. Verandas Events adds a 5% processing fee.
  • Ask three questionsWhich dates are open? What is the guest limit? Is there a cheaper day or a shorter slot? Put the answers in writing before you pay a deposit.
