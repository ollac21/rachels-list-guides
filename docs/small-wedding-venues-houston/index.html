What are the best venues for a micro wedding in Houston?

We do not rank venues; we list the ones that print a small-group price on their own website. The three lowest are The Courthouse Wedding Chapel $197 (2 guests); The Middleton Venue $380 (seats up to 40); The Makenzie House (Little Glass Chapel) $499 (up to 20 guests), with 11 priced venues in all, from $197 to $8,900.

What are some cheap wedding venues in Houston under $1,000?

6 of the 11 venues print a price under $1,000: The Courthouse Wedding Chapel $197; The Middleton Venue $380; The Makenzie House (Little Glass Chapel) $499; Houston Botanic Garden: Global Collection Garden $500; Verandas Events $560; Houston Botanic Garden: Woodland Glade $850. Prices are for the package or hours each venue names, so read the row before you book.

How much does a small wedding cost in Texas?

We do not quote an average. In Houston the venue prices we verified run $197 to $8,900, and the official courthouse fees are $81 for the license plus $100 for a ceremony at Justice of the Peace Precinct 1, Place 1.

Is $5,000 enough for a wedding in Houston?

For a small group it can be: the cheapest verified venue ($197) plus the sample finds comes to about $708, and even the most expensive venue here is $8,900. A 100-guest wedding is a different budget, because this page prices small groups only.
