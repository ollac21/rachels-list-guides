How to pay less for a small venue

  • Book a weekdayMany venues print a lower price for Monday to Thursday. La Venta Inn's ceremony-only package is $4,000 on a weekday and $5,500 from Friday to Sunday. Orcutt Ranch's weekday rental is $900.
  • Ask for the room onlyA package adds an officiant, photos or decor you may not need, and its price shows it. Ask what the room costs on its own.
  • Book fewer hoursA ceremony and a toast fit in an hour or two. Greystone Mansion rents the Formal Garden for a 1-hour ceremony at $2,600. Cute Little Wedding Chapel's lowest tier is $450 and covers the license and ceremony.
  • Ask what comes on topFees, deposits and extra hours can sit on top of the printed price. Orcutt Ranch asks a $500 refundable deposit. Cute Little Wedding Chapel adds $50 on weekends.
  • Ask three questionsWhich dates are open? What is the guest limit? Is there a cheaper day or a shorter slot? Put the answers in writing before you pay a deposit.

What are some cheap wedding venues in Los Angeles under $1,000?

4 of the 7 venues print a price under $1,000: Albertson Wedding Chapel $320; Lucky Wedding Day Chapel $449.99; Cute Little Wedding Chapel $450; Orcutt Ranch: weekday rental $900. Prices are for the package or hours each venue names, so read the row before you book.

What is a small wedding considered?

There is no official cutoff, so we use each venue's own words: the guest limits printed on this page run from 4 to 175 people. Every venue here prints its price for a small, micro, weekday or elopement booking.

How much does a micro wedding cost in Los Angeles?

The venue prices we read run $320 (Albertson Wedding Chapel) to $4,500 (Heritage Square Museum). The low end is a ceremony-only slot; the high end is a package with more included. Food, a dress and rings are extra.

Is $5,000 enough for a wedding in Los Angeles?

For a small group, yes: Lucky Wedding Day Chapel's Sweet Package is $449.99 for 1 to 10 people and Orcutt Ranch's weekday rental is $900 for the first three hours (events up to 175). The most expensive venue here is $4,500 (Heritage Square Museum, a starting price), which alone is most of $5,000. A 100-guest wedding is a different budget; this page prices small groups only.
