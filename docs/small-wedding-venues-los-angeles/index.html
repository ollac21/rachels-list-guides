Questions people ask

What is a normal cost for a wedding venue in Los Angeles?

We do not quote an average, because an average hides what each venue actually prints. On this page 7 venues print prices from $320 to $4,500, each quoted from its own website on Oct 28, 2026.

What are some cheap wedding venues in Los Angeles under $1,000?

4 of the 7 venues print a price under $1,000: Albertson Wedding Chapel $320; Lucky Wedding Day Chapel $449.99; Cute Little Wedding Chapel $450; Orcutt Ranch: weekday rental $900. Prices are for the package or hours each venue names, so read the row before you book.

What is a small wedding considered?

There is no official cutoff, so we use each venue's own words: the guest limits printed on this page run from 4 to 175 people. Every venue here prints its price for a small, micro, weekday or elopement booking.

How much does a micro wedding cost in Los Angeles?

The venue prices we verified run $320 to $4,500. Add the sample finds on this page (a $250 dress, $91 marriage license, $98 flowers, $900 photos) to the cheapest venue and you get about $1,659 before catering, rings and tax.

Is $5,000 enough for a wedding in Los Angeles?

For a small group it can be: the cheapest verified venue ($320) plus the sample finds comes to about $1,659, and even the most expensive venue here is $4,500. A 100-guest wedding is a different budget, because this page prices small groups only.
