How to pay less for a small venue

  • Book a weekdayMany venues print a lower price for Monday to Thursday. La Venta Inn's ceremony-only package is $4,000 on a weekday and $5,500 from Friday to Sunday. Orcutt Ranch's weekday rental is $900.
  • Ask for the room onlyA package adds an officiant, photos or decor you may not need, and its price shows it. Ask what the room costs on its own.
  • Book fewer hoursA ceremony and a toast fit in an hour or two. Greystone Mansion rents the Formal Garden for a 1-hour ceremony at $2,600. Cute Little Wedding Chapel's lowest tier is $450 and covers the license and ceremony.
  • Ask what comes on topFees, deposits and extra hours can sit on top of the printed price. Orcutt Ranch asks a $500 refundable deposit. Cute Little Wedding Chapel adds $50 on weekends.
  • Ask three questionsWhich dates are open? What is the guest limit? Is there a cheaper day or a shorter slot? Put the answers in writing before you pay a deposit.
