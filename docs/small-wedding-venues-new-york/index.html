Questions people ask

What is a normal cost for a wedding venue in NYC?

We do not quote an average, because an average hides what each venue actually prints. On this page 7 venues print prices from $595 to $3,000, each quoted from its own website on Oct 28, 2026.

What are some cheap wedding venues in NYC under $1,000?

2 of the 7 venues print a price under $1,000: Occasions Hall (Queens Party Hall) $595; NYC Wedding Factory $650. Prices are for the package or hours each venue names, so read the row before you book.

What is a small wedding considered?

There is no official cutoff, so we use each venue's own words: the guest limits printed on this page run from 8 to 80 people. Every venue here prints its price for a small, micro, weekday or elopement booking.

How much does a micro wedding cost in NYC?

The venue prices we verified run $595 to $3,000. Add the sample finds on this page (a $250 dress, $150 flowers, $500 photos) to the cheapest venue and you get about $1,495 before catering, rings and tax.

Is $5,000 enough for a wedding in NYC?

For a small group it can be: the cheapest verified venue ($595) plus the sample finds comes to about $1,495, and even the most expensive venue here is $3,000. A 100-guest wedding is a different budget, because this page prices small groups only.
