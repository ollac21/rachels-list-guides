How to pay less for a small venue

  • Book a weekdayMany venues print a lower price for Monday to Thursday. Occasions Hall in Queens is $595 Monday to Thursday, $1,095 on Friday and Sunday, and $1,295 on Saturday.
  • Ask for the room onlyA package adds an officiant, photos or decor you may not need, and its price shows it. Ask what the room costs on its own.
  • Book fewer hoursA ceremony and a toast fit in an hour or two. NYC Wedding Factory sells a 30-minute ceremony for $650. Wave Hill's elopement slot runs up to 90 minutes.
  • Ask what comes on topFees, deposits and extra hours can sit on top of the printed price. Wave Hill's $500 rental fee comes with a required $1,500 Partner Membership. Brooklyn Botanic Garden's $1,000 permit fee is nonrefundable.
  • Ask three questionsWhich dates are open? What is the guest limit? Is there a cheaper day or a shorter slot? Put the answers in writing before you pay a deposit.
