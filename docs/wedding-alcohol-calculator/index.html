Questions people ask

How much alcohol do I need for 100 wedding guests?

For a 4-hour full bar at one drink per guest per hour (400 drinks): about 40 bottles of wine, 8 bottles of liquor (750 ml) and 4 cases of 24 beers. That uses Zola's split of 50% wine, 30% liquor and 20% beer. Add 13 bottles of sparkling if you want a toast.

How many bottles of wine for a wedding of 100?

40 bottles for a 4-hour full bar: 200 glasses at 5 glasses a bottle. If you serve only beer and wine, 60 bottles: Zola's split gives wine 75% of the 400 drinks, 300 glasses.

How many drinks per person at a wedding?

Plan one drink per guest per hour: Zola calls it the typical rule, and Joy's calculator uses it as its "average". Joy's "heavy" setting is two an hour. So a 4-hour reception is about 4 drinks a guest.

How much champagne for a toast for 100 guests?

13 bottles: one flute per guest at 8 flutes a bottle (Zola). 100 divided by 8 is 12.5, rounded up to 13.

How much does wedding alcohol cost?

At September 2026 US average store prices (Bureau of Labor Statistics), wine is about $10.33 a 750 ml bottle and beer $33.75 a case of 24. The wine and beer for 100 guests and a 4-hour full bar come to about $548. Liquor depends on your brand: type its price in the calculator.
