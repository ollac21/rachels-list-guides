Questions people ask

Where can I buy a designer wedding dress for under $500?

Secondhand marketplaces such as Poshmark list designer gowns from individual sellers. On Oct 28, 2026 we found 20 listed at $500 or less, starting at $65 for a David's Bridal gown (seller's original $600).

Is it safe to buy a wedding dress secondhand?

Each listing states its own condition, so read the notes and the seller's photos before you buy. We re-open every listing on Oct 28, 2026 and drop any that is sold or has changed price.

How do I know a secondhand wedding dress will fit?

Sizes on this page are exactly what each seller lists, and bridal sizing often differs from street sizing. Compare the seller's measurements with yours, and tap the dresses you love in Rachel's List so she can watch Poshmark and Etsy for your size every week.

What does "seller's original" mean?

It is the original price the seller typed into the listing, shown exactly as printed. We cannot check what a gown cost new, so we never call it a retail price.

How often is this list updated?

Every Tuesday. We re-open each gown, drop the ones that are sold or repriced, and change the date at the top. The list you see was last checked on Oct 28, 2026.
