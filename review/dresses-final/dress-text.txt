Buying secondhand safely
Measure firstTake your bust, waist and hips, and compare them with the measurements in the listing photos. If a size tag has no measurements, ask the seller.
Know the return rulePoshmark says it gives a full refund if an item never ships or does not match the listing description; otherwise all sales are final. You have 72 hours after delivery to open a return case with photos, so inspect the dress before you accept the order (
Posh Protect
, read Oct 28, 2026).
Ask before you buyMessage the seller for more photos, the fabric, the brand label and tags, and any flaws. A clear answer is a good sign.
Plan for a seamstressGet a quote from a local seamstress before you buy: a hem or a bustle is often priced separately from the dress.
Questions people ask
Where can I buy a designer wedding dress for under $500?
Secondhand marketplaces such as Poshmark list designer gowns from individual sellers. On Oct 28, 2026 we found 20 listed at $500 or less, starting at $65 for a David's Bridal gown (seller's original $600).
Is it safe to buy a wedding dress secondhand?
Condition is described on each Poshmark listing page, not here, so read the notes and the seller's photos before you buy. We re-open every listing on Oct 28, 2026 and drop any that is sold or has changed price.
How do I know a secondhand wedding dress will fit?
Sizes on this page are exactly what each seller lists, and bridal sizing often differs from street sizing. Ask the seller for the bust, waist and hip measurements of the dress and compare them with yours.
What does "seller's original" mean?
It is the original price the seller typed into the listing, shown exactly as printed. We cannot check what a gown cost new, so we show it only as the seller's original price.
How often is this list updated?
Weekly. We re-open each gown, drop the ones that are sold or repriced, and change the date at the top. This list was last checked on Oct 28, 2026.
