# Flow round 1: courthouse (Chicago), first build of FLOW-SPEC

Reviewers (three Sonnet agents, shown the 390 px screens, the full page, the 1440 px screens and the page text).
Scores: answer found in 5 s / would keep reading / trust / would tap the button.

| Reviewer | Answer | Keep reading | Trust | Tap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) engaged bride on a budget | 9 | 6 | 8 | 4 |
| (b) skeptical bride who hates ads | 9 | 6 | 6 | 4 |
| (c) growth / CRO professional | 8 | 7 | 8 | 4 |

## What they asked for (the top changes, by block)
- Offer line (price box): three prices and a Canada line read like a bill or a trial trap. Free first, price second and smaller, under the button; Canada only in the end block.
- Button wording: three different labels (header, bar, block). One label everywhere.
- Dress cards: no photos (we may not use seller photos), $179 to $200 items and a $200 bouquet undercut a $70 wedding. Show the cheapest first, six cards; look for a cheaper bouquet.
- Steps: $15 beside $60 and $10 looks like an $85 total; "official sources" reads like ad copy; the "not shown" items are hidden in the collapsed table.
- Tips: generic. Want a real place, with a source.
- Hero photo costs 250 px of the first screen; hours repeat four times.
- The size chips should carry the size into the app link.

## Changes applied in round 2
Price line under the button, free first, Canada only at the end; one button label; six cheapest dresses by default, size chip adds `&size=` to every button link; step fees only for the parts of the total; sources link names the office; visible "call to check" line; shorter hero; trust line removed (the footer has it); cheaper bouquet and a real photo spot being researched.
