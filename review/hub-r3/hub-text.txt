How to pay less for a small venue
Book a weekdayMany venues print a lower price for Monday to Thursday. Open your city to see each venue's weekday and weekend price.
Ask for the room onlyA package adds an officiant, photos or decor you may not need, and its price shows it. Ask what the room costs on its own.
Book fewer hoursA ceremony and a toast fit in an hour or two. Several venues here sell one-hour and two-hour slots.
Ask what comes on topFees, deposits, taxes and extra hours can sit on top of the printed price. Ask for the total in writing before you pay a deposit.

Questions people ask
What would be considered a micro wedding?
There is no official cutoff, so we use each venue's own words: the guest limits printed next to the 45 prices on our city pages run from 2 to 175 people. Every venue listed prints its price for a small, micro, weekday or elopement booking.
What is a normal cost for a wedding venue?
The middle printed price across the 45 venues on our city pages is $875; they run from $197 (The Courthouse Wedding Chapel, Houston, couple only) to $8,900 (County Line Event Center, Houston, packages for up to 50 guests), each from the venue's own website on Oct 28, 2026.
Is 100 guests a micro wedding?
Usually not: there is no official cutoff, but the guest limits printed next to the prices on our city pages run from 2 to 175 people, and 34 of the 42 limits are 50 or fewer. A venue that hosts 100 guests prices a different kind of day, so we list one only when it prints a small-group price.
What is a realistic budget for a small wedding?
For the venue alone, the prices we verified run from $197 to $8,900. The rest depends on you; our city pages add one real find each for the dress, flowers and photos so you can see how the lines add up.
Is $5,000 enough for a wedding?
For a small group it can be: all 45 venue prices on our city pages are $8,900 or less, before the dress, flowers, photos and food. A 100-guest wedding is a different budget, because these pages price small groups only.
