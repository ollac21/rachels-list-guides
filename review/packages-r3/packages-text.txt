What to ask before you book a package
Is the license included?A package may cover the ceremony but not the marriage license fee. The county sets that fee: see the courthouse guide for your city.
Who is the officiant?Ask if a licensed officiant is part of the price, and whether a short ceremony is the same price as a longer one.
What do the photos include?Look for the number of hours and the number of edited images, and whether you get the files.
What is left out?Flowers, decor, travel, permits and taxes are often priced apart. Ask for the full total in writing.
How many guests, and what if the weather turns?Ask the guest limit and the cancellation and rain plan before you pay a deposit.

Questions people ask
Is eloping cheaper than having a wedding?
On this page the 15 packages print prices from $159 to $4,775, each from the provider's own website on Oct 28, 2026. A package replaces a venue, an officiant and sometimes photos, so compare it with the venue prices on our city pages.
Does eloping make you legally married?
Only the marriage license and a ceremony by an authorized officiant do. A few packages include the license; most do not, so read each card's Includes line. The county license fees we checked run $35 in New York to $91 in Los Angeles (see each courthouse page for the official wording).
What is the average budget for an elopement?
The 13 packages in our five cities run $350 to $4,775, and 7 of them are under $1,000; two Las Vegas chapels are $159 and $249. They include different things, so read the Includes line on each card before you compare.
Is $5,000 enough for a wedding?
For an elopement or micro wedding, yes: every package listed is $4,775 or less before the license fee ($35 to $91) and any travel. A large guest list is a different budget.
What does an elopement package include?
It depends on the provider: some include only a ceremony site and an officiant, others add photos or flowers. Each row on this page names what the provider's own page lists, and the price covers only that.
