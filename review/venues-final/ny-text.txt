How to pay less for a small venue
Book a weekdayMany venues print a lower price for Monday to Thursday. Occasions Hall in Queens is $595 Monday to Thursday, $1,095 on Friday and Sunday, and $1,295 on Saturday.
Ask for the room onlyA package adds an officiant, photos or decor you may not need, and its price shows it. Ask what the room costs on its own.
Book fewer hoursA ceremony and a toast fit in an hour or two. NYC Wedding Factory sells a 30-minute ceremony for $650. Wave Hill's elopement slot runs up to 90 minutes.
Ask what comes on topFees, deposits and extra hours can sit on top of the printed price. Wave Hill's $500 rental fee comes with a required $1,500 Partner Membership. Brooklyn Botanic Garden's $1,000 permit fee is nonrefundable.
Ask three questionsWhich dates are open? What is the guest limit? Is there a cheaper day or a shorter slot? Put the answers in writing before you pay a deposit.

Questions people ask
What is a normal cost for a wedding venue in NYC?
The middle price of the 7 venues printed on this page is $2,000, and 2 of them are under $1,000 ($595 to $3,000 overall). A price covers only what the venue names: its hours, days and guest limit, so read the card before you compare.
What are some cheap wedding venues in NYC under $1,000?
2 of the 7 venues print a price under $1,000: Occasions Hall (Queens Party Hall) $595; NYC Wedding Factory $650. Prices are for the package or hours each venue names, so read the row before you book.
What is a small wedding considered?
There is no official cutoff, so we use each venue's own words: the guest limits printed on this page run from 8 to 80 people. Every venue here prints its price for a small, micro, weekday or elopement booking.
How much does a micro wedding cost in NYC?
The venue prices we read run $595 (Occasions Hall (Queens Party Hall)) to $3,000 (Bartow-Pell Mansion: Conservatory). The low end is a ceremony-only slot; the high end is a package with more included. Food, a dress and rings are extra.
Is $5,000 enough for a wedding in NYC?
For a small group, yes: Occasions Hall in Queens is $595 Monday to Thursday (hall only, bring your own food), and the most expensive venue on this page is $3,000 (Bartow-Pell Mansion: Conservatory, up to 30 people, 3 hours). Either leaves part of $5,000 for food, a dress and flowers. A 100-guest wedding is a different budget; this page prices small groups only.
